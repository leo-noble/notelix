How it works
01
Upload your PDFs
Drop one or multiple PDF lecture slide files. No sign-up, no server — everything runs privately in your browser.
02
Select & arrange slides
Pick which slides to keep, remove extras, and edit individual pages with the built-in per-slide editor.
03
Configure enhancements
Invert dark slides, clear backgrounds, strip logos, apply grayscale, and choose how many slides fit per page.
04
Export & download
Get a clean, print-ready PDF instantly. Perfect for printing notes or studying offline.
